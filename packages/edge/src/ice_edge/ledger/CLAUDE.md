# Ledger (`ice_edge.ledger`)

Scoped `CLAUDE.md`, loaded when working under `packages/edge/src/ice_edge/ledger/`. Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md`; project-wide rules stay there.

**FIFO method attribution:** `escrow_credits` is a FIFO ledger of `Credit(method, cents, ts)` that backs `credit_escrow_cents`, which remains the authoritative total (integer cents; see Money below). Deducting a sale's price consumes credits first-in-first-out (`EscrowLedger.consume_fifo`) and records the per-method shares on `pending_sale_shares`; `vend_failed` restores **exactly those shares as separate credits with their original methods**, so money is never reclassified when a sale fails. Method strings are stored raw from `PaymentEvent.method` and classified only at query time. If the ledger and the total ever disagree the sale is booked to `{"unknown": price}` with a warning — a bug guard, not a path. The ledger itself lives in `packages/edge/src/ice_edge/ledger/escrow_ledger.py`'s `EscrowLedger` (the second piece carved off the VMC god object, after `FaultRegistry`); `credit_escrow_cents`/`escrow_credits` stay public read/write conveniences on the VMC over the ledger's `total`/`credits` (the divergence guard inside `EscrowLedger.consume_fifo` still only ever sees them move together), and `escrow` is the public read-only `@property` over the `EscrowLedger` object itself for callers that want it (e.g. `vmc.escrow.is_empty_within_tolerance`).

**Session evidence:** the whole lifecycle of `session.json` as crash evidence now lives in `packages/edge/src/ice_edge/sale/session_evidence.py`'s `SessionEvidence` (owned by `Machine`, exposed as `machine.evidence`; `machine.recovery` is an alias), not in `ledger`: the hold flag (`hold`/`release`/`held`, wired into `StatusOutputs` and `DispenseCycle` by `Machine`), `recover_at_boot()` (called by `Machine.set_session_store` after the store attaches: `evaluate_at_boot()` decides `discard_test`/`uncertain`/`closed`/`none`, then it seeds the gateway's dedupe, logs, flags an uncertain session with PAY-104, or re-queues a closed snapshot's cash_sale), the read-only `recovery_detail()`/`pending_sale_for_recovery()`/`plain_clear_refused()`, the operator actions `record_pending_sale(recorder)` (Record as sale) and `discard()` (one `asyncio.Lock` held across each check-write-clear span; the route maps their `RecordOutcome`/`DiscardOutcome` to HTTP), the in-memory record-once guard and durable marker, and `on_fault_change`, which re-queues the boot snapshot's payouts when PAY-104 clears (`Machine._on_fault_change` calls it first, before the reconciler). `Machine`'s `recovery_detail`/`pending_sale_for_recovery`/`send_recovered_cash_sale`/`pending_sale_already_recorded`/`reserve_pending_sale`/`mark_pending_sale_recorded` remain one-line forwards for now. The `is_test` chokepoints are `evaluate_at_boot` and `pending_sale_for_recovery`. Sale-already-recorded matching uses the injected `sale_recorded` (wired to `EventRecorder.sale_recorded_since`, so attach the recorder before the session store): an open snapshot whose sale already has a row is cleared as `closed` only when no credit, pending payout or read error is left; the match window is `dispense_timeout_seconds + SALE_MATCH_MARGIN_SECONDS` (480 s; 600 s at the default). `recovery_detail()` feeds the chips on the PAY-104 row of `health_faults.html`; there is deliberately no "Refund leftover" action (after a restart the credit is no longer in edge escrow, and once PAY-104 resolves edge-start reconciliation pays the board's excess out as an `error` payout).

**Clearing DATA-101 for a pre-cents journal row:** the row stays in `sales-journal.jsonl` until an operator handles it by hand (record the sale from its dollar values or remove the line, keeping a copy). `DATA-101` is raised again on every boot until then; `DATA-*` faults never block payment.
